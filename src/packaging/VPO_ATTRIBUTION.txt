VIRTUAL PLAYING ORCHESTRA 3 - SAMPLE CREDITS
============================================

The orchestra sounds in Orchestrium come from Virtual Playing Orchestra (VPO),
a free orchestral sample library created by Paul Battersby:

    https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/

Orchestrium redistributes VPO unmodified and free of charge, as VPO's terms
allow. VPO and its source material are free and must stay free: do not sell
this library, in part or in whole.
